# AI job titles: search demand, 2022-2026

Charts for a LinkedIn post on US Google search demand across 1,052 AI job titles.

- `post-a-prompt-engineer-peaked.png` (1080x1080) - prompt engineer peaked June 2023, context
  engineer grew 96x, ai engineer overtook both
- `post-b-tool-not-job.png` (1080x1080) - 71% of the volume is people shopping for AI tools,
  not researching careers
- `post-c-invented-vocabulary.png` (1080x1350) - 662 of 1,052 titles have no search demand

Source: Google Ads Keyword Planner API, United States, Aug 2022 - Jul 2026, retrieved
2026-07-29. Volumes are rounded buckets, not exact counts.

Run `Rscript charts.R` from this directory. Data pipeline: github.com/papageorgiou/ai-jobs
